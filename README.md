# Kelsi Raben Portfolio

Working portfolio site for Kelsi Raben.

## About

This portfolio highlights selected marketing work across professional services, B2B manufacturing, and professional sports.

The site is designed to tell a cohesive story of my experience across strategy, branding, content, digital marketing, fan engagement, community relations, and hands-on execution.

## Site Structure

- `index.html` — Main portfolio homepage
- `pacific-press.html` — Pacific Press Holdings case study
- `otters.html` — Evansville Otters case study
- `kemper.html` — Kemper CPA Group case study
- `content.js` — Editable homepage project content and links
- `styles.css` — Global website and case-study styling
- `script.js` — Site functionality
- `assets/` — Portfolio imagery, resume, headshot, and project files

## Current Case Studies

### Pacific Press Holdings

A five-month brand implementation project focused on translating a new identity across three related brands and applying it to machines, marketing materials, physical spaces, product collateral, and FABTECH.

### Evansville Otters

A sports marketing and fan engagement case study focused on creating a different game-day experience each night through promotions, content, community engagement, sponsorship activation, staff management, and live execution.

### Kemper CPA Group

A professional services marketing case study focused on helping build a more structured, consistent, and growth-oriented marketing function across a multi-office, multi-division organization.
