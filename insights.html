Insights

How I think.
How I work.
Why it creates value.

I’ve worked across manufacturing, professional services, and sports marketing. The industries changed, but the way I approached the work kept repeating: understand people, make complexity clearer, connect ideas to larger goals, and carry good thinking all the way through execution.

The through-line

I tend to work in the space between people, ideas, and execution.

04 — Why Marketing Fits

Marketing lives
in the overlap.

People. Ideas. Clarity. Creativity. Business. Execution.

The reason marketing has continued to fit me is that it rarely asks for only one kind of thinking.

It requires understanding people while still understanding the business. It asks for creativity that can support a strategy. It rewards curiosity, communication, judgment, and the ability to make an idea tangible.

That combination is where I do my best work.

Across my experience, the recurring theme has been bridging the gap between people, ideas, and execution—translating complexity into clarity, building trust, and turning thoughtful ideas into action.
